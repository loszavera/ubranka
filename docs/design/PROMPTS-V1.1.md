## A — Krem i śliwka

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high-fidelity mobile UI concept and button/component presentation board for the Polish virtual stylist app Ubranka. Create a landscape 1536x1024 visual design board with ample clean margins. Left: a large flat front-on mobile viewport, no perspective, no physical phone, showing the complete top-to-bottom outfit-selection screen. Right: a neatly aligned component panel with primary and secondary button samples, selected style chip, focus ring and disabled state, plus 4 palette swatches. This is a design proposal for approval, not a screenshot of implemented functionality. All text in Polish with accurate accents. Header above mockup outside app identifies the concept letter and name and a small label "Propozycja wyglądu". In-app brand "ubranka", main heading "Dwa zestawy dla Ciebie", small style chips "Codzienny" and "Elegancki", budget chip "Do 500 zł". Exactly TWO outfit proposal cards side by side. Each card contains a realistic tasteful fully clothed fictional adult person wearing a complete casual-elegant outfit on a quiet studio backdrop. Use the same fictional adult woman in both cards, neutral natural body proportions, no measurements or body-shape judgement, no before-after body edits. One outfit navy trousers and cream knit, the other beige trousers and dark blazer; shoes visible. These are illustrative generated outfit visualizations, not actual merchandise or a real user's photo. No real retailer logos. Card labels "Zestaw 1" and "Zestaw 2", prices "329 zł" and "459 zł", small badge "Z Twojej szafy" on one garment item. Each outfit has its own large easily distinguishable rejection and like controls, an outlined "Nie" and a filled heart/like control; do not imply like means buying. A small line under the cards says "Podoba mi się = poznajesz mój styl". Bottom navigation has 3 items: "Zestawy", "Szafa", "Profil", with Zestawy active. Right component panel labels "Przyciski" and includes full-width primary button with exact text "Podoba mi się", secondary "Nie dla mnie", tertiary text "Pomiń", a subtle disabled example "Zapisz", and a sample text input. Buttons have legible dark-on-light or white-on-dark contrast and large touch targets. No red/green voting gimmicks, no neon, no flashy gradients, no pricing plans, no unnecessary dashboards. Keep the two proposals and the controls prominent and the board very polished. Prioritize visual usability, calm hierarchy and exact readable Polish copy.

Concept header: "A — Krem i śliwka".
Design direction: Warm soft ivory background #F7F3EE, deep muted plum #553C50, pale lavender-gray surfaces, charcoal text. Friendly premium typography with a substantial humanist sans-serif headline, rounded 20px cards and softly rounded buttons. Warm, welcoming, calm, inclusive of women and men. Subtle fine borders and almost no shadows.
```
